Who is traveling?

Child fares are half the adult fare (rounded down to the nearest ¥10), and child passes cost about half too — the same rules the official pass uses. Children under 6 ride free and don't need a pass.

The same pass is sold at two prices. The official website is currently selling at the pre-increase prices as a limited-time offer (end date not announced), while authorized sellers charge the standard prices — a gap of ¥3,000–5,000 per pass. That gap is larger than this checker's ±¥2,000 "too close to call" band, so where you buy can flip the verdict. Pick your route and the math below follows it.

How the verdict works

  • We add up the individual ticket price of every ride for your whole party.
  • For each pass length (7, 14, 21 days), we try every possible start date inside your trip and keep the one that covers the most value.
  • If the best pass saves you more than ¥2,000, the verdict is BUY. If it costs more than ¥2,000 extra, it's SKIP. In between, it's a TOSS-UP.
